A Skyward Tradition

An October tradition in Albuquerque’s high desert, framed by the Sandia Mountains. The Fiesta began in 1972 with 13 balloons at Coronado Center for KOB’s 50th anniversary; a world championship followed in 1973. It moved to today’s field in 1996. [1]

Our history

Dawn Patrol, mass ascensions in two waves on selected mornings, and glows or drone shows on selected evenings bring different views of the Fiesta. Explore the Discovery Center and live entertainment on the ground. All activities depend on the session and weather. The “Albuquerque Box” uses different wind directions at different heights—only when conditions cooperate. [2, 7]
