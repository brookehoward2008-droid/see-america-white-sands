THE BEGINNING

A small gathering.
A skyward tradition.

An October tradition in Albuquerque’s high desert, framed by the Sandia Mountains. In 1972, 13 balloons gathered at Coronado Center for KOB’s 50th anniversary. A world championship followed in 1973; the Fiesta moved to today’s field in 1996. [1]
